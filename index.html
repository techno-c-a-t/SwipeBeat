Features

Everything you need to sort music

Swipe Controls

Swipe up to approve a track, swipe down to skip. Instant response with tactile vibration feedback.

Smart Jump

Skip long intro silence automatically. Fast forward by seconds or percentage to hear the song's core.

History & Undo

Open history anytime with one click. Undo mistakes easily by swiping sideways.

Sources & Duplicates

Select local folders or existing .m3u playlists. Smart filtering removes unwanted duplicates.

M3U / M3U8 Export

Auto-detects character encoding (UTF-8) so your playlists render properly on any external player.

100% Offline & Private

Works exclusively with local files. No internet access, no accounts, no ads, no trackers.
